# What / How / Why (Example)

| # | WHAT | HOW | WHY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I'm at home, wrapping up some work. I realize I'm late to class and rush to campus. | Anxious, rushed | I lost track of time while working.
| 2 | I leave home 30 mins before class (my normal commute time). When transferring trains, the next train comes in 15 mins. | Extreme rage | I didn't know when trains were delayed or undergoing maintenance, so I didn't account that in my commute.
| 3 | I have a meeting right before class but it goes over schedule. I rush to end the call so I can head to class. | Awkward, stressed | I don't block off time for communting because I don't have scheduling boundaries.
