# Synthesis

Everyone I talked to had been late to something recurring in the last month, but it wasn't carelessness. One interviewee manages three class times a week entirely from memory, no calendar, no alarm, nothing written down, and she's been burned by it before without ever changing her approach. What surprised me: she doesn't see this as a system failing her, she describes it as just how she operates. The real problem isn't that people forget, it's that they don't treat forgetting as something worth defending against until it's already too late.

# How Might We
How might we help people defend against forgetting before it's too late, instead of after?

# Select and Commit
A lightweight tool that watches your calendar and fires a warning before each scheduled transition, not after you're already running late. It removes the need to notice the time yourself, catching the moment your own attention doesn't.
